A note on the foundations

Intelligence is a dynamical process, not a function.

Frontier models memorize human knowledge with extraordinary fidelity, yet must be explicitly taught every skill they perform. Knowledge is not intelligence. We are developing a new class of model in which adaptation, memory, and computation are not training-time artefacts but properties of the substrate itself.

Premise

Humans know very little. We use it extremely well. The opposite of today's transformers, which know nearly everything and use it only as instructed.

Approach

Intelligence as a dynamical system — small units capable of memory, computation, and adaptation, composed into larger systems whose collective behaviour is adaptive reasoning and self‑correction.

Status

Concept to working models in four months. The framework is real.
